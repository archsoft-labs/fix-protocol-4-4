# FIX 4.4 : OwnerType <522> field

**Type:** [int](https://www.onixs.biz/fix-dictionary/4.4/index.html#int)

## Description

Identifies the type of owner.

Valid values:

1 = Individual Investor

2 = Public Company

3 = Private Company

4 = Individual Trustee

5 = Company Trustee

6 = Pension Plan

7 = Custodian Under Gifts to Minors Act

8 = Trusts

9 = Fiduciaries

10 = Networking Sub-Account

11 = Non-Profit Organization

12 = Corporate Body

13 = Nominee
