# FIX 4.4 : TrdType <828> field

**Type:** [int](https://www.onixs.biz/fix-dictionary/4.4/index.html#int)

## Description

Type of Trade.

Valid values:

0 = Regular Trade

1 = Block Trade

2 = EFP (Exchange for Physical)

3 = Transfer

4 = Late Trade

5 = T Trade

6 = Weighted Average Price Trade

7 = Bunched Trade

8 = Late Bunched Trade

9 = Prior Reference Price Trade

10 = After Hours Trade
